Arrange

Very Frequent

Verb

手配する、準備する

Definition

To organize or plan something.

Example

We need to arrange a meeting with the client.

顧客との会議を手配する必要があります。

Synonyms

Organize · Schedule

Collocations

Arrange a meeting · Arrange transportation

Usage Notes

Arrange is commonly used when organizing events, meetings or practical matters.
